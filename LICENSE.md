## Bundled audio samples

The Grand Piano instrument shipped in `public/samples/grand-piano/` is a
subset of the **Salamander Grand Piano V3** sample set:

- **Author:** Alexander Holm
- **Source:** https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano
- **License:** [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)

The subset ships 30 note zones across the full 88-key range (sampled in
minor thirds, MIDI 21..108) at 2 velocity layers, recorded on a Yamaha C5
concert grand with two AKG C414 microphones in an AB pairing (48 kHz /
24-bit FLAC). Redistribution and public deployment are permitted under
CC BY 3.0 with attribution, as stated in the instrument manifest
(`public/samples/grand-piano/manifest.json`) and in
`public/samples/grand-piano/ATTRIBUTION.txt`.

**Attribution:** "Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm (CC BY 3.0)."
